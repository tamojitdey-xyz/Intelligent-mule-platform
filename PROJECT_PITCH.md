# Competition pitch outline

## Problem
Mule accounts may be used as intermediaries in fraudulent fund flows. Investigators need to prioritize activity and understand account relationships, not just inspect isolated transactions.

## Proposed solution
Intelligent Mule combines explainable transaction risk indicators, account-level summaries, relationship visualization, investigation case tracking, and an audit trail to help analysts decide what to review next.

## Validation plan
Use synthetic or authorized anonymized data. Build labelled test scenarios, report precision/recall and false-positive rates, compare against a baseline, and document thresholds. Do not claim accuracy until measured on an independent, suitable dataset.

## Roadmap
Add investigator authentication and roles, CSRF protection, temporal graph features, model evaluation, encryption, data-retention controls, tamper-evident logs, and a documented human review process.
